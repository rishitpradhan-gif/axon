From samples to spectrum

What happens to the signal

Samples are shown as recorded, in µV. No filtering, resampling, interpolation, or re-referencing is applied. Classic EDF values use the declared physical/digital calibration. Acquisition prefilters remain part of the input.

Welch power density

The FFT segment length is the largest power of two at or below the chosen duration × sample rate, available window length, and 8,192 samples. Each segment is mean-centered and multiplied by a periodic Hann window. Segment overlap is 50%. Modified periodograms are averaged; interior one-sided bins are doubled, DC and Nyquist are not. Units are µV²/Hz. Segments touching missing samples are excluded. The exported settings give the actual duration, frequency spacing and unused tail.

Reading the plots

The trace uses min–max envelopes when many samples share a display bin; bins with missing data appear as gaps. Vertical clipping is a display limit, not a modification to samples. Spectra are plotted in dB relative to 1 µV²/Hz, with a −120 dB display floor. Band power sums PSD × frequency spacing for bins in [low, high). A band above Nyquist or containing no frequency-bin centers is unavailable, not zero. No confidence intervals, significance tests, or brain-state interpretations are computed.

Quality and provenance

Threshold excursions, ADC rails (EDF only), near-constant samples and missing data trigger review flags. These do not establish artifacts or clinical findings. Exports include file SHA-256 (or demo seed), settings, spectral values, pinned comparison, and notes. They contain no raw samples and cannot reconstruct a recording. Keep the original file separately. Filenames, labels and notes are included; review before sharing.
